JPKI Local Signer digitally signs PDF documents with Japan's My Number Card (マイナンバーカード), entirely on your device.

Hold your card to your iPhone (NFC) and enter your signing PIN, and the app embeds a detached CMS/PKCS#7 signature (adbe.pkcs7.detached) into your PDF — the format accepted by Japan's registry e-filing system (登記・供託オンライン申請システム). The card's private key never leaves the card: the signature is computed on-card and only the signature value is returned.

The app can also verify signed PDFs offline — checking the signature value, document integrity, and certificate-chain trust (JPKI root certificates are bundled) — and co-sign documents with multiple signers.

Privacy by design:
• No servers, no cloud, no accounts.
• No network access at all — no networking APIs are linked into the app.
• Your PIN, hashes, and signature values are never logged or saved.
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Requirements: an NFC-capable iPhone (iOS 17+) and a physical My Number Card holding a valid signing certificate. Verification works without a card.

Note: This app generates signatures; it does not perform revocation checks (CRL/OCSP). Whether a signed PDF is accepted by a given filing system is the user's responsibility to confirm.

Open source (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/nunnun/jpki-local-signer
